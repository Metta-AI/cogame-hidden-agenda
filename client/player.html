Hidden Agenda — seat page

This page is informational. A Hidden Agenda policy is a PROMPT: the seat process connects to /player, delivers its PLAYER_PROMPT (or PLAYER_SCRIPTED=miner|lurker) once, and then only listens. Every decision is made inside the game container, which is what lets all five seats be asked in one parallel batch at each decision point.

This page never opens the player websocket. The certifier fetches it before the player pods start, so opening a seat socket here would take the slot the real seat needs.

Watch the game at /client/global.
